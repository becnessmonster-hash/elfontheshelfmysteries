Case #974

The Case of the Vanishing Toy Train

Closed

When Santa’s favorite toy train disappears from the workshop, it’s up to our elves to track it down before Christmas Eve!

Reveal the solution

The Culprit

The prototype itself. During final testing, an accidental burst of North Pole magic triggered its unfinished self-drive system, sending it racing through the workshop unsupervised.

Resolution

The train was stopped safely, upgraded with a proper control switch, and later became one of the season’s most popular toys.

Its original prototype remains carefully stored after hours.

Case #973

The Heist of the Golden Sleigh Bell

Closed

The Golden Sleigh Bell is not just decorative — it is one of the oldest magical artifacts at the North Pole, rung only once each year before Santa’s departure. When it disappeared during final sleigh checks, the entire hangar went into lockdown.

Initial evidence suggested someone familiar with workshop security: no forced entry, no broken seals, and only one unusual clue — a single silver feather caught in the sleigh rail.

Investigators traced faint drag marks upward into the rafters, where a hidden nest had been built above the maintenance lights.

Reveal the solution

The Culprit

A young snow owl had taken the bell after being drawn to its shine and sound. It had carried the bell into its nest, collecting other shiny objects from around the hangar along the way.

Resolution

The bell was recovered undamaged and returned before Christmas Eve departure. The owl was safely relocated, and hangar staff introduced new security rules for magical artifacts during maintenance.

Case #972

The Mystery of the Frosty Footprints

Closed

Strange footprints are found leading away from the reindeer stables—who’s been sneaking around in the snow? For five consecutive mornings, oversized frozen footprints appeared around the village perimeter. Each trail began at the forest edge and ended near the frozen lake, with no sign of who — or what — had made them.

No gates were disturbed, and no one reported sightings.

After fresh snowfall revealed a full overnight trail, investigators discovered the tracks circled the village, paused near the Christmas lights, and disappeared back toward the hills before dawn.

Reveal the solution

Investigation Objectives

  • Identify the Origin of the Footprints: Determine who or what left the strange footprints.

  • Investigate Potential Threats: Assess any potential risks or threats posed by the presence of these footprints.

  • Ensure Reindeer Safety: Secure the reindeer stables and prevent any further incidents.

Resolution

The investigation concluded with the identification of Jingle as the source of the mysterious footprints. The elf’s actions were found to be harmless, though unintentional. The reindeer stables were secured, and Jingle was given a formal warning and instructed to follow proper procedures for accessing the reindeer area in the future.

Case #971

The Great Candy Cane Caper

Closed

One morning, the candy cane factory alarm bells rang. Thousands of candy canes had vanished overnight.

The factory floor was covered with red-and-white crumbs, and a peppermint scent filled the air.

Investigators followed a sticky trail through the workshop, across the reindeer stables, and all the way to the snow-covered forest.

Reveal the solution

The Culprit

A group of hungry snow bunnies had discovered a hole in the candy storage wall.

They had been carrying candy canes away to decorate their burrows for the holidays.

Resolution

The North Pole engineers fixed the wall, and the elves kindly delivered a special crate of mini candy canes for the snow bunnies each winter.

Everyone agreed: sharing candy is better than stealing it.

Case #970

The Shadow of the North Pole Syndicate

Closed

A powerful underground syndicate is threatening to disrupt Santa’s entire operation. Our top investigators must infiltrate the dark side of the North Pole, outwit the elusive leader, and dismantle the network before Christmas is thrown into chaos.

Reveal the solution

The Culprit

A group of rookie elves had formed a “secret club” to practice their detective skills. Unfortunately, their sneaking around caused chaos in the workshop.

Resolution

Instead of being in trouble, the elves were invited to join the Junior North Pole Detective Training Program.

The Syndicate was officially disbanded… but their detective careers were just beginning.
