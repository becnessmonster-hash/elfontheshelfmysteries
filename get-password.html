The Elf Did It

Each Elf had an alibi, so the police had the six come down to the station for a line up. Elf Sparklefoot easily identified the guilty elf and he was locked up behind bars to await trial. From the clues provided can you determine the position of each elf in the line up, where the police found him, and his alibi?
